Make a chart from a spreadsheet in three steps

Select the cells you want in Excel or Google Sheets, copy them, and paste them into the box above. The chart updates as you type. When it looks right, download it as a PNG for a slide, a report, or a post.

Where does my data go?

Nowhere. The chart is drawn inside your browser and nothing is sent to a server.

Can I paste straight from Excel or Google Sheets?

Yes. Tabs, commas and semicolons all work, and numbers like $1,200 or 15% are understood.

Which chart should I pick?

Bars compare categories, lines show change over time, and pies show shares of a whole when there are only a few slices. Pie and doughnut charts use the first column of numbers, and scatter charts use the first column as the x values.
